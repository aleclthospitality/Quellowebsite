Quello, a luxurious Italian dining destination from acclaimed New York City restaurateur Joe Campanale.

Set in a renovated farmhouse in historic Belle Haven, Quello draws inspiration from the sun-drenched traditions of the Italian coast, with fresh crudo, handmade pasta, and spritzes at sunset.

Quello is centered on warm hospitality, exceptional cuisine, and a wine program built from the finest producers in Italy and beyond.
